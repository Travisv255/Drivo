The Problem

Buying and selling a car in Tanzania still runs on trust you can't verify.

Most transactions happen through informal networks, with no accountable party standing behind the vehicle, the price, or the payment.

No accountable seller

Buyers deal with individual owners or informal agents, with no company standing behind the vehicle's condition or history.

Unsafe payment flow

Money and paperwork often change hands directly between strangers, with no neutral party managing the transaction.

Inconsistent pricing

Vehicle valuation is negotiated case-by-case, making it hard for either side to know if a price is fair.

The Solution

Drivo is the seller — not just the listing.

Owners hand their vehicle to Drivo. We inspect it, value it, and take on the sale ourselves — Drivo becomes the seller of record, not a middleman connecting two strangers.

Buyers pay Drivo directly. There is one accountable company behind every price, every inspection, and every handover.

  • One company behind every sale — not an anonymous listing
  • Buyer pays Drivo directly; no informal cash handovers
  • Pricing and terms are set and controlled by Drivo
  • Every vehicle inspected before it's listed
How It Works

From owner to buyer, in five steps.

01

Acquisition

Owner brings their vehicle to Drivo and signs an agreement to sell through the platform.

02

Valuation

A professional inspection sets a fair, verified price for the vehicle.

03

Listing

The vehicle appears on the Drivo app as a Drivo-owned listing, not a third-party post.

04

Sale

Buyer selects the vehicle and pays Drivo directly through the app.

05

Delivery

Drivo coordinates handover and paperwork with the buyer.

Market Opportunity

A large, fragmented market with no dominant trusted brand.

Vehicle ownership in Tanzania is expanding alongside a growing middle class and rising smartphone use, yet no single company has become the trusted name for buying or selling a used car.

Existing options are split between informal dealer yards and unmoderated classifieds — leaving space for a brokerage that owns the full transaction.

Business Model

Revenue comes from the resale margin.

Drivo acquires a vehicle at an assessed value, then sells it at a margin that covers inspection, listing, and handling.
